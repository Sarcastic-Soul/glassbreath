      How your data is handled

      • Camera: only on after you press “Turn on camera”, and off when you press “Turn off camera” or close the app. The camera light shows when it is on.
      • Where processing happens: the Presage SmartSpectra SDK measures breathing, pulse, blinks and expressions on this computer. Video frames are never uploaded or saved.
      • Network: the SDK contacts Presage only to check the API key.
      • Usage counts: off by default. If you turn it on, the SDK sends Presage one anonymous summary per session (SDK version, OS, frame counts, error codes). Never video, never readings.
      • What is stored: nothing, unless you press “Keep on the rack”. Then a picture of the vessel and its shape are saved in this app's folder on this computer. Delete them any time from the Cooling rack.
      • Not medical: breathing and pulse are rough camera estimates used to shape art. They are not health advice.
